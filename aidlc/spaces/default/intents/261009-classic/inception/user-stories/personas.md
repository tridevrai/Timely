# Personas

## P1: The Owner (primary, only active user in version 1)

- **Role**: the single person who plans and does the work in Timely; also the
  developer and approver of the app itself. [Q1 A, requirements FR1.2]
- **Goals**:
  - Get every open task out of their head so it stops causing background anxiety.
  - Turn the day into a few time-blocked commitments that actually get finished.
  - Protect long focus stretches from distraction and constant messages.
- **Pain points**:
  - A "massive list" that feels impossible and leads to avoidance.
  - Tasks written too broadly to start ("Work on taxes").
  - Drifting between shallow tasks and keeping chat and email open all day.
  - Losing time to tweaking settings in complex apps ("productivity theater").
- **Context**: works on a Mac and an iPhone; uses Google Calendar; plans each
  morning and works in Pomodoro intervals during the day. [requirements NFR1, FR7]
- **Tech comfort**: high.
- **Frequency**: daily; target is planning at least 5 days a week. [requirements FR12]

## Non-user: People the owner delegates to

People named on delegated tasks do not use Timely in version 1. They appear only
as a free-text name with a follow-up date. Accounts for them are a later phase
and get their own persona then. [requirements FU1 C]
